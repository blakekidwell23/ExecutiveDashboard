Why these numbers won't match the other sales reports: dollars here are credited by role, not by full order value. A rep who quotes AND processes an order gets 100% credit. If they only quoted it (someone else processed), they get 80%. If they only processed an order someone else quoted, they get 20%. Processing an online order also earns 20%. This is intentional — it's meant to reflect who actually did the work, not just who's attached to the order — but it means the $ figures on this report will not tie out to Location Sales Performance, Sales-to-Plan, or any other report that counts full order value. Treat this report as a read on individual rep activity and trend, not as a company revenue reconciliation.
