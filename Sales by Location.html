This looks less like an extraction error on our end (company-wide totals are unchanged before/after the reclass, and two independent totals in the source file cross-check exactly) and more like something worth a second look at the source: was a large one-time account or order retroactively assigned to Fresno for WK32 2025 specifically? Recommend confirming with whoever ran the reclass before treating Fresno's new WK32 number as final.

Standouts & Concerns

▲ Growing

  • Santa Fe Springs (SFS) — +21.0% YTD, and it's not a fluke: +21.1% in July, and a confirmed +64.6% for the complete WK32 — the strongest single-week number of any location last cycle. Part of this is inherited volume from the closed CLX warehouse, but this is a bigger jump than the trend alone would predict; worth watching whether it holds.
  • San Diego (SD) — +11.6% YTD, accelerating: +24.0% in July, +35.2% for the complete WK32. The strongest sustained momentum of any location — also picking up some of CLX's former volume.

▼ Declining

  • Albuquerque (ABQ) — the clearest concern. Down in all three windows: -13.3% YTD, -20.9% July, -20.2% for the complete WK32. This isn't a one-week dip, it's a sustained decline.
  • Fresno — now down in all three windows too, but see the territory-reclass flag above before treating this as a new trend. -6.3% YTD, -11.2% July, and a sharp -41.2% for the complete WK32 (was roughly flat before the Aug 12 Hayward→Fresno reclass: +0.3% YTD, -12.0% July, -1.9% WK32). July and YTD moved moderately; WK32 moved dramatically and asymmetrically between the two comparison years — flagged above as needing a second look, not read as a real 41% single-week drop yet.
  • Hayward (HAY), the biggest location by volume — improved on two of three windows after the reclass: WK32 -2.5% (was -7.7%) and YTD now +2.2% (was +1.3%), though July stayed roughly flat at -18.0% (was -17.6%). Worth watching given its size — it's the largest single driver of the company total — but the reclass did what Blake expected here.
  • Sacramento (SAC) — unaffected by the reclass, still +8.9% YTD despite a slightly negative complete WK32 (-2.1%); not a new concern.

⚠ WK33 is 2 of 5 days in (Aug 10–11) and still very early: HAY's pace reads -27.1% and ABQ -30.6%, while SD reads +30.5% and Fresno +32.3% — all likely to move as the week fills in. None of this changes the Standouts/Concerns above, which are based on the complete WK32 + July + YTD; treat WK33's early numbers as noise until more of the week lands.
