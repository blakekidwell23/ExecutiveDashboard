Standouts & Concerns

▲ Growing

  • Santa Fe Springs (SFS) — +21.0% YTD, and it's not a fluke: +21.1% in July, and +41.8% (rate/day) so far in WK32. Consistent growth across all three windows — part of this is inherited volume from the closed CLX warehouse.
  • San Diego (SD) — +11.6% YTD, accelerating: +24.0% in July, +38.0% (rate/day) so far in WK32. The strongest recent momentum of any location — also picking up some of CLX's former volume.
  • Sacramento (SAC) — +8.9% YTD, and WK32 is up so far too (+2.8% rate/day), though July (-4.9%) cooled — too early to call WK32's uptick a trend reversal.

▼ Declining

  • Albuquerque (ABQ) — the clearest concern. Down in all three windows: -13.3% YTD, -20.9% July, -23.9% (rate/day) so far in WK32. This isn't a one-week dip, it's a sustained decline.
  • Hayward (HAY), the biggest location by volume — down -17.6% July, -11.8% (rate/day) so far in WK32, though still +1.3% YTD. Worth watching given its size — it's the largest single driver of the company total.
  • Fresno — flat YTD (+0.3%) but a soft July (-12.0%); WK32 is actually up so far (+16.4% rate/day) but it's only 2 days of data, worth watching before reading into it either way.
