Why these numbers won't match the other sales reports: dollars here are credited by role, not by full order value. A rep who quotes AND processes an order gets 100% credit. This is intentional — it's meant to reflect who actually did the work, not just who's attached to the order — but it means the $ figures on this report will not tie out to Location Sales Performance, Sales-to-Plan, or any other report that counts full order value.
